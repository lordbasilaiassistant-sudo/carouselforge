03 / FAQ

The fine print, briefly.

Is it really free?

Yes. The entire designer runs in your browser — paste points, pick a theme, export every slide as a high-res PNG, no signup and no watermark. Pro mode is optional and only kicks in if you bring your own GLM key for AI-written copy.

Where does my content go?

Nowhere. Slides are drawn locally with HTML canvas; your text never leaves the device. The only network call happens if you opt into Pro mode, in which case your prompt goes directly from your browser to the official z.ai API using your own key.

What can I export?

Square 1080×1080 or Portrait 1080×1350, rendered at full native resolution. Grab the whole deck as a single .zip, or download slides one at a time.

Do I need an API key?

No. Writing your own copy is free forever. A key is only needed if you want the AI to draft the slides for you, and you bring your own — it is stored only in this page.
